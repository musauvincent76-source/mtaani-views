Frequently asked questions

Is registration free?

Yes. Joining Mtaani Mashabiki Hub is completely free.

Why can't I register again?

Each phone number can be registered once, to keep the leaderboard fair. If you registered with the wrong team, contact the admin to have it fixed.

What happens to my number?

The admin saves it as a contact so you can be added to the community. It is not shown publicly on this website.

How does this get me more views?

WhatsApp shows your Status to people who have saved your number. The more members who save you, the bigger your audience.

How do I get everyone's contacts?

The admin shares a contact file (.vcf) in the WhatsApp group. Open it on your phone and tap Import to save all members at once.

Will I see their Status too?

Yes. When you save the members, you can see their Status updates as well, and they can see yours.

Do I have to support a team?

No. The team is only for the fun leaderboard. Choose "Other" if your team is not listed.

How can I leave the community?

Send a message to the admin and your details will be removed.
